- Krafton announced plans to invest an additional $250 million in India over the next three to four years, targeting AI, robotics, and deep tech.
- The new commitment raises Krafton's total planned investment in India to over $500 million, complementing the $670 million India-focused growth fund with Naver and Mirae Asset announced in December 2025, to which Krafton contributes around $137 million at first close.
- Krafton has backed about 18 Indian companies, including gaming studios and startups like Nodwin Gaming, Loco, Pratilipi, Kuku FM, and One Impression, and acquired Nautilus Mobile in 2024.
- Krafton plans to launch BGMI Lite in India by the end of 2026.
