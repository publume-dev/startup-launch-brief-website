- ChatNest is an AI support chatbot builder that lets users create, train, and embed a chatbot on any website without code, as announced on Product Hunt.
- The chatbot can be trained via pasting business info, adding FAQs, setting rules, or crawling a URL, and it answers only from its knowledge base to avoid fabrication.
- ChatNest offers one-line embed for HTML, WordPress, Shopify, React, and Webflow, and includes a live inbox with human takeover.
- The product has a free plan without credit card requirement and provides WordPress and WooCommerce plugins.
