- Radar is a podcast search engine from Particle that transcribes audio and understands meaning to extract quotes and highlights.
- Radar transcribes over 130,000 podcasts, including Apple's Top 200 in 135 verticals, with 20,000 episodes added daily.
- It provides entity tracking, alerts via email/Slack/webhook, and a podcast ads search engine.
- Pricing is $29 per month per seat, with a $399-per-month business plan, and custom API pricing.

According to CEO Sara Beykpour, hedge funds are the highest-volume customers integrating with Radar's API.
