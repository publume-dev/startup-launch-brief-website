- Blue Voice, a Boston-based AI startup providing real-time policy guidance to police officers, has come out of stealth with $6 million in funding led by SignalFire and Las Olas VC.
- The platform is used daily by officers at 225 county agencies across 25 states, answering a question every minute.
- Blue Voice is trained on department-specific laws, local ordinances, protocols, and guidelines not accessible to general-purpose AI tools, and points officers to original regulations rather than generating answers on its own.
- Over the last year, Blue Voice grew its customer base elevenfold, according to CEO David Lawrence.
- The startup recently developed functionality to help detectives solve cold cases.

Notably, Blue Voice's effectiveness claims (e.g., reduced crime, fewer controversies, contribution to preventing a kidnapping) are self-reported by the company and not independently verified; pricing, deployment requirements, and revenue figures have not been disclosed.
