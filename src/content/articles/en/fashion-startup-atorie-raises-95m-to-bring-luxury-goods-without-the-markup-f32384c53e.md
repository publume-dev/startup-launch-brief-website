- Atorie announced a $9.5 million seed round with investors including a16z speedrun, Night Capital, and Lightspeed Ventures' Jeremy Liew.
- Atorie sells handbags and clothes made from the same materials and factories as high-end manufacturers, with an Italian leather handbag costing a few hundred dollars compared to thousands from luxury brands.
- Atorie's AI analyzes fashion trends, estimates consumer demand, and predicts material shortages, while its consumer-facing AI agent helps build outfits and learns shopping habits.
- Atorie ended last year with around $5 million in sales and expects to reach an annualized run rate north of $55 million this year.
- The company plans to use the fresh capital to fund logistics, build more AI tools, support production, release its own in-house line, and work with creators to launch their own clothing lines.
