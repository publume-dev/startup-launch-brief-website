- Tabs, founded in 2023 by Ali Hussain, provides an AI platform that automates revenue recognition, billing, and collections.
- The company has raised approximately $92 million in total capital and reached a $400 million valuation in its latest funding round.
- Tabs employs about 180 people and reports triple-to-quadruple year-over-year revenue growth.
- It raised a $4 million pre-seed round co-led by Primary Venture Partners and One Way Ventures.
