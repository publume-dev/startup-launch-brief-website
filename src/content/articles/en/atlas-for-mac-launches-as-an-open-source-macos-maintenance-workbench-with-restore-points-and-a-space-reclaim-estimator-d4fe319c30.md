- Atlas for Mac is an open-source macOS maintenance workbench that scans first, lets users review items, logs every action with a restore point, and allows restoration within a retention window.
- It recognizes dev toolchains such as Homebrew, Xcode, and Node, and excludes them from cleaning by default.
- The team launched a free web-based Space Reclaim Estimator that estimates how much space can be reclaimed before installing anything.
- Caveat: These claims are self-reported by the product team via Product Hunt; no independent reviews or third-party validation are provided.
