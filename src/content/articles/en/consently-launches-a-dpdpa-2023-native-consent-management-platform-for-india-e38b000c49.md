- **Capabilities:** It provides cookie consent, purpose-based consent, and data principal rights management.
- **Language support:** It supports 22 Indian languages.
- **Pricing/availability:** It is available to start free.
- **Caveat:** All details are self-reported on the Product Hunt launch page and lack independent third-party validation; the "India's first" claim is not independently verified.
