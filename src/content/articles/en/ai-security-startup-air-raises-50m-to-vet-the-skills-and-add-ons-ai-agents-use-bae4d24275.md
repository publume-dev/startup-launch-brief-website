- AIR emerged from stealth with $50 million raised across two seed rounds: $10 million led by Sequoia and $40 million led by Greenoaks.
- The platform discovers AI agents running in companies, continuously vets their skills, tools, and components, and blocks interactions that fail security criteria.
- AIR reports filtering out about 27% of add-ons and skills found online, with more than 20 customers, roughly a quarter of which are large enterprises.
