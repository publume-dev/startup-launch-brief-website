- Rillet raised a $100 million Series C at a $1 billion valuation, led by Iconiq, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia.
- The platform uses AI to help finance professionals manage company books, automatically pulling data from sources like Salesforce and Brex.
- Rillet has over 600 customers and doubled its annual recurring revenue (ARR) in the past three months.
- Including prior funding, Rillet has raised more than $200 million to date, with a $70M Series B and $25M Series A.
