- ClearJet raised a $25 million Series B led by Edison Partners, bringing total funding to $40 million since its founding in 2022.
- ClearJet connects shippers with unused cargo capacity on commercial flights, claiming shipping cost reductions of up to 35% and delivery speed improvements of one to three days.
- ClearJet reports profitability, revenue more than tripled year over year, approaching nine figures, and moves over 30 million packages annually across 95 U.S. airports.
