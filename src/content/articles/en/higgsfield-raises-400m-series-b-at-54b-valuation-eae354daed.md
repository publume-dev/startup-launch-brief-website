- Higgsfield announced a $400 million Series B at a $5.4 billion valuation, led by DST Global with participation from Goldman Sachs Alternatives, Valor Capital, and Tribe Capital.
- The round comes eight months after Higgsfield's previous valuation of $1.3 billion.
- The company claims $700 million in annualized revenue, 30 million users across 200 countries, and partnerships with 390 Fortune 500 companies.
- Products include Cinema Studio for filmmakers and Marketing Studio for marketing teams; founder is former Snap exec Alex Mashrabov.
- Revenue and user figures are self-reported and not independently verified.
