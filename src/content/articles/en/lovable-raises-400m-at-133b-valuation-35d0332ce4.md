- Lovable raised $400 million in a Series C led by Menlo Ventures and Scaleup Europe Fund at a $13.3 billion valuation.
- The previous round was $330 million at a $6.6 billion valuation in December, also led by Menlo Ventures with CapitalG as co-lead.
- The company reports reaching $500 million annualized run rate revenue in June 2026 and hosting 60 million projects with 900 million monthly visitors.
- Lovable signed a multiyear deal with Google Cloud in June, a fivefold increase in usage, and offers its own in-house trained AI model.
- Financial and usage figures are self-reported and not independently verified.
