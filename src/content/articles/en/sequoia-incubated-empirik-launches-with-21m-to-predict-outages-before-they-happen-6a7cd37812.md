- Empirik launched as an independent company after raising $21 million in seed funding from Sequoia, Canapi, and Alumni Ventures.
- Empirik provides an autonomous infrastructure engineer that tracks system changes and infers their potential ripple effects to predict outages before they occur.
- Empirik has early customers including Guardant Health and a major consumer packaged goods company.
- Kartik Chandrayana, former Quantum Metric CPO and Salesforce observability VP, leads Empirik as CEO.
- Claims about Empirik autonomously preventing outages and being a new category are self-reported by Sequoia partner and CEO; independent validation is not yet available.
