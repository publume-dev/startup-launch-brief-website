- Ultrahuman raised $70M in a funding round including Qualcomm Ventures, at a $365M valuation.
- Qualcomm and Ultrahuman are working together on a ring powered by Qualcomm silicon, enabling on-device software.
- Software updates are scheduled for delivery by end of September 2026, enabling game controller and AI interaction features on existing Ring Air and Ring Pro devices.
- The company reports an annual revenue run rate of $140M, expects $200M by January 2027, and has sold roughly 800,000 rings.
- Ultrahuman may not be profitable this year due to investments in physical expansion, branding, and clinical research.
