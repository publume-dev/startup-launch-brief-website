- Neko Health will open its first US office at 300 Lafayette Street in SoHo, New York on September 24, 2026.
- More than 25,000 New Yorkers have joined a waitlist for Neko Health's US launch.
- Neko Health's technology combines proprietary body scans, bloodwork, and fitness device data, and it currently operates in the UK and Sweden.
- Neko Health raised a $700 million Series C last month and reported that 100,000 people had already received scans.
