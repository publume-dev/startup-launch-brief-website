- Clipto raised $15 million in an all-equity round at a $250 million post-money valuation, with investors including HSG, GL Ventures, EnvisionX Capital, Palm Drive Capital, Hans Tung, Lu Zhang, and 522 Ventures.
- Clipto indexes videos, audio, images, meetings, and other files locally on a user's device, and users can search by describing what they need or ask AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude to access data within user-defined scope via MCP support added about two weeks ago.
- Clipto reports more than 30 million people have used its products since launch, hundreds of thousands of paying subscribers, and reached $15 million in annual recurring revenue at the beginning of 2026, while remaining profitable on a net-income basis, according to founder Henry Kang.

The adoption and revenue figures are self-reported by the company and lack independent verification; the founder declined to provide precise subscriber count or average revenue per customer.
