- Cecilia Ziniti, former general counsel at Replit, founded GC AI, a legal AI startup targeting in-house legal departments.
- GC AI has raised nearly $72 million across three rounds, including a $60 million Series B co-led by Scale Venture Partners and Northzone at a $555 million valuation.
- GC AI serves roughly 2,100 companies, including Lockheed Martin, Time, Eventbrite, Vercel, and Gusto, and grew 400% year over year.
