- Félix announced $200 million in Series C funding co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and General Catalyst.
- A16z led an $87 million equity investment with participation from QED Investors, Castle Island Ventures, Switch Ventures, Contour Venture Partners, and Endeavor Catalyst.
- General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund committed $113 million in debt.
- Félix has raised nearly $300 million in total since its founding in 2020.
- The company's valuation has increased threefold since its $75 million Series B in 2025 (exact valuation undisclosed).
- Félix reports processing over $8 billion in transactions to date.
- Félix reports revenue growth of more than 2.5x in the past year.
- Félix serves 11 Latin American markets including Mexico, Brazil, Costa Rica, Honduras, and Peru.

Félix plans to use the new capital to expand offerings and enter new markets across Latin America.
