- Letara has raised ¥2.6 billion (~$16 million) in new funding.
- The round was co-led by Headline Asia, JIC Venture Growth Investment, and Incubate Fund, with participation from NES, Toyoda Gosei, and Frontier Innovations.
- Letara plans to expand from hybrid satellite thrusters to developing large hybrid rocket systems for space, defense, and security markets.
- Letara has already won orders from rocket and satellite companies as well as the Japanese government.

- The company's next major milestone is an in-orbit firing test with an overseas partner.
