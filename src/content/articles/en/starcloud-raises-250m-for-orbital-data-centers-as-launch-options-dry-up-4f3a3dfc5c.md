- Starcloud raised a $250 million extension to its March Series A, valuing the company at $2.3 billion, according to TechCrunch.
- The round was led by Manhattan West Ventures, with participation from Nvidia, Cisco, Benchmark, EQT, and others; a source familiar with the deal said Nvidia invested $25 million, which the company did not confirm.
- Starcloud plans to launch two 8 kW compute satellites (Starcloud-2) on rideshare flights in 2027 for orbital inference tasks, with customers including U.S. government agencies.
- It is the only known company operating an Nvidia H100 GPU in orbit and the first to train a model using it; it is sharing data with Nvidia as it develops the Vera Rubin Space-1 chip.
- The company has requested FCC permission to operate 88,000 spacecraft.

Starcloud has not yet secured specific launch contracts for its larger Starcloud-3 spacecraft planned on Starship; the timeline depends on Starship's successful demonstration.
