- According to Meta's Product Hunt launch, Pocket is a new social app for creating, playing, and remixing AI-powered interactive experiences.
- Users can describe an idea to turn it into a playable 'gizmo' that responds to touch, motion, sound, photos, and the camera.
- Creators can share their gizmos to a feed and remix others' creations, with no coding required.
- Caveat: These are self-reported claims from Meta; specific details on availability, pricing, or adoption metrics are not provided, and independent validation is pending.
