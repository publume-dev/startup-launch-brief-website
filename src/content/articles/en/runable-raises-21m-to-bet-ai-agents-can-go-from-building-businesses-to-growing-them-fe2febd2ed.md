- Runable raised a $21M Series A co-led by Susquehanna Venture Capital and Nexus Venture Partners, with participation from Together Fund and Array VC, at a $65M post-money valuation.
- The AI agent enables natural-language creation of websites, apps, and presentations, and is extending to run ad campaigns, manage social media, handle SEO, and optimize AI chatbot presence.
- The company reached a $2M annualized revenue run rate within three weeks of launching payments in March, according to co-founder and CEO Umesh Kumar.
- Runable has about 1.7 million registered users, with the U.S., UK, and Japan among its largest markets.
- In a TechCrunch test, Runable built and deployed a website and prepared an ad campaign for a fictional coffee subscription business, but required an external ad account to run the ads.
- Runable's ability to run ads without customers connecting their own advertising accounts is currently available for ads on ChatGPT, according to the company.

Runable's expansion from building to marketing could shift how small businesses adopt AI, though its revenue claims remain unverified.

Watch for details on ad platform partnerships and real-world validation of growth features, especially beyond ChatGPT ads.
