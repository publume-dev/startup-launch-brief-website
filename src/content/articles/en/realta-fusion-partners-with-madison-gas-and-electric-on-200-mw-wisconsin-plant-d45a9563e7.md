- Realta Fusion announced a deal with Madison Gas and Electric to explore building a 200-megawatt fusion power plant in Wisconsin by the mid-2030s.
- Madison Gas and Electric made an equity investment in Realta Fusion, though specifics were not disclosed.
- Realta Fusion gains access to interconnection sites and receives engineering and technical assistance from the utility.
- Commonwealth Fusion Systems is partnering with Dominion Energy to lease land for its Arc power plant, expected to generate 400 MW near Richmond, Virginia, in the early 2030s.
- Helion is working with Chelan County Public Utility District in Washington State for its first power plant, Polaris, intending to bring the 50 MW facility online in 2028 to provide electricity to Microsoft.
- Type One Energy is planning a 350 MW power plant on the site of a former coal plant near Oak Ridge, Tennessee, as part of a deal with the Tennessee Valley Authority, with electricity supply expected in the mid-2030s.
- Proxima Fusion is planning to build its first commercial power plant, Stellaris, on the grounds of a decommissioned fission plant in southern Germany, with RWE as an investor and aiming to turn it on in the late 2030s.
