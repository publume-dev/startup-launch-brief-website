- Keenable raised $26 million in seed funding led by Accel, with participation from Conviction Partners and unnamed business angels.
- Keenable says it has built a web search index of over 100 billion documents and its API is already used in production by several undisclosed AI labs and inference providers during both training and runtime.
- Keenable recently formed a partnership with voice AI company Gradium to support live information retrieval.
- Keenable is developing a proprietary retrieval product called WebQueryLanguage, designed to help AI systems answer questions by combining information from various web sources even when none contain the full answer.
- Keenable plans to double its headcount of 15 engineering staff by the end of the year to build its go-to-market motion.
