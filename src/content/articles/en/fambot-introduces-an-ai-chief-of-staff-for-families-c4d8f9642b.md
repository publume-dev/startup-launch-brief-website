- Fambot is described as an 'AI chief of staff' for families, connecting email, calendar, and WhatsApp to deliver a daily checklist and proactive look-ahead.
- It offers a web and mobile app interface in addition to text.
- Founded by former Instagram engineer Greg Karlin (CTO), David Reich (CEO), and Jason Morrow.
- Backed by $3.5 million in pre-seed funding co-led by NextView Ventures and Baukunst.
- Currently in free beta on iOS, Android, and web, with over 1,000 families having tested it.
