- Pie launched Community Homes, allowing users to create permanent group homepages with community feeds, admin controls, and event planning tools.
- The app has grown to around 300,000 users, backed by investors including Forerunner, Lightspeed, Accel, and angels like Ev Williams.
- After changing the activity feed in December to highlight who you know attending events, time on the app grew from 2 minutes to 10 minutes.
- Pie plans to add group chats and a feature for casual connection bids like 'hang out and craft together'.

Adoption and retention metrics for Community Homes are not yet available; the update is new.
