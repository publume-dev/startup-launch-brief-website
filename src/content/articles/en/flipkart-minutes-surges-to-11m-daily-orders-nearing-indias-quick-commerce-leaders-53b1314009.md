- Flipkart Minutes is delivering 1.1 million to 1.2 million orders per day, up from 390,000 to 400,000 in November, according to sources familiar with the matter.
- Operates about 1,020 to 1,050 micro-fulfillment centers, up from 600 in January and 340 a year ago, with plans to reach 1,500 by end of 2026.
- About 65% to 70% of customers are repeat buyers, and transactions per customer have increased 50% to 60% year-over-year.
- Average delivery time has decreased to about 11 minutes from 13 minutes a year ago.
- These figures are sourced from unnamed individuals and may not be independently verified.
