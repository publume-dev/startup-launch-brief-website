- Liux has secured Europe-wide homologation for its Liux Big microcar and expects to start selling in the first half of 2027.
- The Liux Big features a linen-based biocomposite body designed for recyclability, easy-to-replace batteries, and a 240-liter trunk, with two battery versions planned: 15 kWh and 20 kWh.
- Liux targets a price below €18,000 before EV subsidies and has over 7,500 people on its waiting list, with most prospective buyers aged 55-60 in urban areas.
- The company has raised €16 million (about $18.5 million) so far, including European funding, and plans to sell through European car dealership partnerships.
- Liux operates three production facilities in Spain, including one in Azuqueca de Henares, and claims production capacity could reach 20,000 cars per year by 2030.
