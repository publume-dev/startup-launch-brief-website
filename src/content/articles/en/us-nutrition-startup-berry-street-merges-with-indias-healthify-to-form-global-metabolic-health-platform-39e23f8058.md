- Berry Street and Healthify have merged, with Berry Street founder Noah Kotlove and Healthify founder Tushar Vashisht as co-CEOs. 
- In the U.S., the company operates under the Berry Street brand; in other markets like India, it operates under the Healthify brand. 
- Berry Street has a network of over 2,000 clinics and offers insurance-covered GLP-1 care. 
- Healthify claims to have served over 45 million customers, while Berry Street claims to have served over 200,000 people in collaboration with Amazon, Walmart, and other platforms. 
- Healthify has raised over $150 million in funding per Crunchbase, and Berry Street raised $50 million last year from Northzone, Sofina, and FJ Labs.
